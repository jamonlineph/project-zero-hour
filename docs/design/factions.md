# Factions

Four sides, each with a distinct way to win. Rosters are in [units-and-buildings.md](units-and-buildings.md); powers are in [commander.md](commander.md).

"X-class" in these docs names the real-world system a unit is based on, for the designers' reference. **In-game names are always generic or invented** ([../06-legal-and-licensing.md](../06-legal-and-licensing.md)).

## United States and partners (`us`): expensive and precise

- **Fantasy:** a small, networked force that sees everything and strikes from far away.
- **Strengths:** sensors, precision long-range fires, stealth aircraft, drone wingmen.
- **Weaknesses:** expensive units, a slow economy early on, and heavy dependence on spotters.
- **Signature mechanics:**
  - **Kill chain:** long-range weapons fire only at targets a spotter has marked (recon drone, sniper team, satellite pass). Kill or jam the spotter and the shot is lost.
  - **Active protection** on tanks: a few charges that shoot down incoming missiles and drones.
  - **Shoot and scoot:** rocket artillery relocates automatically after firing.
  - **Drone wingmen:** unmanned jets that escort each fighter.
- **Economy flavor:** logistics trucks can be upgraded to drive themselves (faster, no crew needed).
- **Look:** angular hulls, sensor domes, sand and olive.

## China (`cn`): mass and layers

- **Fantasy:** an industrial giant that floods the field with drones behind an air-defense umbrella.
- **Strengths:** cheap mass production, drone swarms, layered air defense, long-range rockets.
- **Weaknesses:** individual units are weaker, and the army is vulnerable while it's still building up.
- **Signature mechanics:**
  - **Mass production:** repeat builds in a queue get cheaper and faster.
  - **Swarm launchers:** trucks that release drones in batches.
  - **Linked air defense:** air-defense sites strengthen each other when their coverage overlaps.
  - **Robot dogs:** armed quadruped robots, cheap and expendable, and vulnerable to jamming.
  - **Navigation satellite station:** a building that boosts guided-weapon accuracy. Destroying it hurts.
- **Look:** smooth slab armor, grey-green, digital camouflage.

## Russia (`ru`): artillery and jamming

- **Fantasy:** grind the enemy down with artillery while their drones fall out of the sky.
- **Strengths:** massed artillery, glide bombs, electronic warfare, tough tanks, field fortifications.
- **Weaknesses:** slow, predictable, and hurt by mobile enemies and fiber-optic drones.
- **Signature mechanics:**
  - **Jamming fields:** blind enemy drones and guided weapons. **Spoofing** turns enemy drones on their own side.
  - **Fortifications:** engineers dig trenches, tank obstacles and minefields.
  - **Counter-battery radar:** enemy artillery is revealed when it fires.
  - **Decoys:** inflatable tanks and air-defense sites.
  - **Cage armor:** tanks shrug off the first drone hits.
- **Look:** boxy hulls, add-on armor and cages, dark green.

## The Irregulars (`irr`): cheap and mobile (fictional)

- **Fantasy:** a fictional network of private contractors and volunteers who fight with what they can buy, build or repurpose.
- **Strengths:** cheap, fast and mobile, with drones that can't be jammed and no power grid.
- **Weaknesses:** fragile units, no manned aircraft, no heavy armor beyond refurbished old tanks.
- **Signature mechanics:**
  - **No power** needed.
  - **Mobile base:** buildings pack up and move.
  - **Contracts:** cash bounties for map objectives, with three on offer at a time.
  - **Fiber-optic drones** that ignore jamming.
  - **Decoy positions.**
- **Hard rules:** not based on any real group. No terror tactics, no attacks on civilians, no suicide units. They fight like a private military force.
- **Look:** mismatched panels, civilian vehicles, tan and rust.

## Later nations (after 1.0)

Candidates: India, a European defense force, Turkey. Each gets a design pass with the same template: fantasy, strengths, weaknesses, signature mechanics and look.

## Sub-faction variants (after 1.0)

Each faction gets 2–3 variants with roster changes and bonuses, chosen before a match. Examples:

| Faction | Variants |
|---|---|
| United States | Armored, Expeditionary (air-mobile), Unmanned (drone-heavy) |
| China | Rocket Force, Swarm, Combined Arms |
| Russia | Artillery, Electronic Warfare, Airborne |
| Irregulars | Drone Workshop, Mercenary, Partisan |
