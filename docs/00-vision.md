# Vision

## Elevator pitch

**Signal Front** is a fast base-building real-time strategy game set in 2026. You build a base, gather supplies, climb a tech tree and earn commander powers, then fight a war where:
- drones fill the sky,
- jammers blind them,
- every long-range strike needs a spotter.

It plays as well on a phone as on a Googlebook or a PC.

## Design pillars

1. **Readable, fast battles.** Matches last 15–30 minutes. Every unit's job is obvious at a glance, and every threat has a visible counter.
2. **2026 warfare.** Drones, counter-drone weapons, jamming, sensor networks and interceptable strikes are core mechanics, not decoration.
3. **Asymmetric factions.** Four sides that play differently, not recolors of each other.
4. **Touch-first, mouse-perfect.** Designed for touch from day one, with full mouse, keyboard and trackpad support for Googlebook and PC.
5. **Open and moddable.** Open-source code and assets. Units, weapons and maps are data files anyone can edit.

## Inspiration and the IP boundary

The game is inspired by classic 2000s base-building RTS games, with *C&C Generals: Zero Hour* as the main influence. We reuse **genre mechanics**, which nobody owns, and **nothing else**.

**Allowed:**
- builder units, supply harvesting, power grids
- garrisoning buildings, transports with firing ports, stealth and detection
- veterancy, commander ranks with a power tree
- superweapons with public countdowns
- asymmetric factions and sub-faction variants
- skirmish, challenge-ladder and campaign modes

**Not allowed:**
- names, factions, characters, story, missions or text from any existing game
- specific unit, building or power designs from other games, including renamed or "modernized" versions
- art, models, textures, sounds, voices, music, maps, UI layouts or logos
- any file taken from a game install or a mod

The rosters in [design/units-and-buildings.md](design/units-and-buildings.md) are original, drawn from real 2026 militaries. When in doubt, ask in `tasks/QUESTIONS.md`.

## What 1.0 contains

**Factions:** four.
- United States and partners
- China
- Russia
- the **Irregulars**, a fictional private-military network

**Per faction:** about 15–20 units and 12 buildings, commander ranks 1–5 with powers, and one superweapon.

**Content and modes:**
- 10–12 maps, for 2 to 4 players
- skirmish against the AI (3 difficulties, several personalities)
- LAN and online multiplayer, replays
- Gauntlet mode: a ladder of AI commanders
- A short campaign of "operations", which can slip to after 1.0

**Platforms and languages:**
- Platforms: Android phones and tablets, Googlebook, Chromebook, Windows, Linux, macOS.
- Languages: English at launch, then community translations.

## Non-goals for 1.0

- Naval warfare; nuclear, chemical or biological weapons.
- Real-world terrorist groups, real ongoing conflicts, real political leaders, attacks on civilians.
- Microtransactions, loot boxes, ads.
- iOS. It may come later; GPL apps on the App Store are a separate legal question.
- Real trademarked product names in the game. Design docs may say "X-class" for reference only.

## Who it's for

- Players who loved 2000s RTS games and want one on their phone or laptop.
- Newcomers who want a readable modern-warfare strategy game.
- Modders who want an open, data-driven game to build on.

## Name

"Signal Front" is a working codename. The final name is picked after a trademark search (backlog P0-10), before any public release.
